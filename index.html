OUR STORY

A celebration
of global flavours.

Located on the 4th floor of Avalon Arcade in AECS Layout, The Spice Studio brings a world of culinary traditions to your table. We built this space to be more than just a restaurant—it is a venue for connection, celebrations, and great food.

With our expansive 160-seater layout, we comfortably host casual diners and large groups alike. Our chefs use the finest ingredients to craft dishes that cater to every palate, ensuring a memorable dining experience for both vegetarian and non-vegetarian food lovers.
